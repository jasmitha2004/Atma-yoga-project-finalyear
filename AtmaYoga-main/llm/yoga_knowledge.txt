Balasana (Child’s Pose):
Promotes deep relaxation and mental calmness by gently stretching the back and hips.
Helps slow down the nervous system and release stress.
Avoid if you have knee injuries.

Baddha Konasana (Bound Angle Pose):
Helps release tension from the hips and lower body where stress is commonly stored.
Encourages relaxation and emotional balance.
Avoid if you have severe hip or knee pain.

Pawanmuktasana (Wind-Relieving Pose):
Relieves fatigue by improving digestion and blood circulation.
Reduces heaviness in the body and refreshes the mind.
Avoid if you have recent abdominal surgery.

Salamba Bhujangasana (Supported Cobra Pose):
Gently stimulates the spine and opens the chest, increasing oxygen intake.
Helps overcome sluggishness without exhausting the body.
Avoid if you have severe back pain.

Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward-Facing Dog):
Calms the mind by increasing blood flow to the brain.
Reduces anxiety and helps regain emotional stability.
Avoid if you have wrist or shoulder injuries.

Janu Sirsasana (Head-to-Knee Forward Bend):
Encourages introspection and emotional release.
Soothes the nervous system and helps reduce feelings of sadness.
Avoid if you have lower back injuries.

Ustrasana (Camel Pose):
Opens the chest and heart region, helping release suppressed anger and frustration.
Promotes emotional openness and mental clarity.
Avoid if you have neck or spine problems.

Vajrasana (Thunderbolt Pose):
Improves focus and mental stability.
Helps ground the mind and calm racing thoughts.
Avoid if you have severe knee pain.

Paschimottanasana (Seated Forward Bend):
Relaxes the mind and reduces emotional heaviness.
Helps release stress stored along the spine.
Avoid if you have slipped disc or severe back pain.

Navasana (Boat Pose):
Improves concentration and mental strength.
Helps channel anxious energy into controlled focus.
Avoid if you have back or neck strain.
